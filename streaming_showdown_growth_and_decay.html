📖 The Formula Lab

💗 Linear function

General form

f(x) = mx + b

Our model

m = constant rate of change (slope); b = starting value (y-intercept).

🌿 Exponential function

General form

f(x) = a · bx

Our model

a = starting value; b = growth factor. For a signed rate r%, b = 1 + r/100. Rates below 0% represent exponential decay.

A negative rate represents a decrease. If a model predicts a negative price, it is no longer realistic in this context.

In these models, t represents years since signup, and the output is the monthly subscription price—not the total paid.
